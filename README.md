# Supplement Sales Dashboard – Amazon, Walmart, and iHerb

## Executive Summary

### Objective
This project aims to analyze and visualize supplement sales performance across three major platforms—**Amazon**, **Walmart**, and **iHerb**—by tracking key performance indicators such as revenue, pricing trends, product returns, and geographic contributions.

---

## Data Source

- **Dataset:** Supplement_Sales_Weekly_Expanded.csv
- **Time Period:** January 2020 to April 2025 (Weekly Data)
- **Dimensions Covered:**
  - Product Name, Category, Platform, Location, Week
- **Metrics:**
  - Units Sold, Units Returned, Revenue, Price, Return %

> The dataset captures weekly sales data for a range of health and wellness supplements across several categories including **Protein**, **Vitamins**, **Omega**, **Amino Acids**, and more.  
> Sales data is collected from three e-commerce platforms—**Amazon**, **Walmart**, and **iHerb**—spanning geographic markets in the **USA**, **UK**, and **Canada**.

## Dashboard Features & Insights

### 1. Revenue and Pricing by Category
- **Top Performing Categories**: Vitamin, Mineral, and Performance supplements.
- **Highest Priced Category**: Omega supplements show premium pricing.
- Both **volume-driven** and **price-driven** product categories are identifiable.

### 2. Revenue by Platform
- Distribution is balanced:
  - **Walmart:** $7.86M (34.28%)
  - **Amazon:** $7.67M (33.47%)
  - **iHerb:** $7.39M (32.25%)
- All platforms are performing comparably, indicating consistent demand across retailers.

### 3. Geographic Performance
- **USA** leads in total revenue, followed by **Canada** and **UK**.
- The map and flow chart visualizations offer insights into regional product preferences and market depth.

### 4. Product-Level Insights by Region
- Regional differences in product performance can be used to design geo-targeted strategies.
- Products like **Collagen**, **Creatine**, and **Ashwagandha** are top contributors in multiple regions.

## Conclusions & Recommendations

- Focus marketing on top-performing categories like **Vitamin** and **Performance** supplements.
- Evaluate pricing strategies, especially for high-value categories like **Omega**.
